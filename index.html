Prasiddha Shakya (Sid)

I am an applied microeconomist and Strategic Research Fellow in the Division of Strategy and Policy at the University of North Carolina System Office. I hold a PhD in Economics from Colorado State University, where my dissertation focused on the economics of education. My research examines how education and labor-market policies shape opportunity, student success, and longer-run economic outcomes.

My work has appeared in Education Finance and Policy, Economics of Education Review, Applied Economics Letters etc. I use administrative data and quasi-experimental methods to study questions involving teacher labor markets, college access and affordability, student decision-making, and worker outcomes. More details and current drafts are available on my Research page.

My current and future work explores the relationships among education, labor markets, and the environment. I am especially interested in how institutions and policies shape educational pathways, employment decisions, and the distribution of environmental risks.
